# vLLM 0.27.1 on the FP8 27B, decode versus depth

## What was measured

`vllm.container` pins `docker.io/kyuz0/vllm-therock-gfx1201:latest`, locally
three months old, and the recorded vLLM depth figures come from it. A newer tag,
`rocm7.14.0-torch2.11.0-vllm0.27.1` (image created 2026-08-12, revision
`c5dd87e`), was already pulled but never run. The question was whether vLLM's
depth falloff — the reason it lost to the daily driver at every depth in
[runs/2026-08-30-engine-decode-depth](2026-08-30-engine-decode-depth.md) — has
improved upstream.

**Method.** The shipped unit was shadowed into
`~/.config/containers/systemd/users/vllm.container` with only the image tag
changed, in both `Image=` and the `ExecStartPre` pull check. Everything else is
the baked launcher: `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8`, TP=2, MTP k=4 on `TRITON_ATTN`,
prefix caching with `mamba-cache-mode align`, `--max-model-len 131072`,
`gpu_memory_utilization` 0.80, `NCCL_PROTO=Simple`. lemonade was stopped first;
both cards read 57 MiB before the start.

**Speculation was on.** The engine logged
`speculative_config=SpeculativeConfig(method='mtp', ..., num_spec_tokens=4)` and
reported draft acceptance between 66.7% and 73.9% across the run, so this is a
speculating arm and comparable to the daily driver's MTP figures.

**Two starts are still required.** The first start died in
`_check_enough_kv_cache_memory` with 4.74 GiB needed against 4.02 GiB available
and an estimated maximum model length of 109,344. The second start, on the
compile cache the first had filled, came up in ~80 s with 6.31 GiB available.
This reproduces the cold-compile shortfall recorded in
[runs/2026-09-13-fused-gdn-kernel-27b-fp8](2026-09-13-fused-gdn-kernel-27b-fp8.md),
in a different image and a different vLLM version.

## What it means

**The depth falloff is genuinely better upstream.** The gain is zero on short
prompts and grows monotonically with depth, reaching 48% at 69,751 tokens. That
is the shape of a fix to the deep-context path rather than a general speedup, and
it is the specific weakness that decided against vLLM here.

**It still loses to the daily driver at every depth.** The gap at 69,751 narrows
from 2.61x to 1.76x, and 1.42x at 37,763. Closing, not closed.

**Context, not throughput, is what rules it out for this box.** The whole KV pool
is 174,274 tokens and `max_model_len` is 131,072, against lemonade's 262,144 per
slot. The sessions this box actually runs sit at 150–170K
([runs/2026-09-18-kv-quant-and-slot-count](2026-09-18-kv-quant-and-slot-count.md)),
so vLLM would refuse them outright, and at its own maximum length it holds 1.33
of them. The speed comparison is secondary to that.

**The headroom to test it properly exists but was not taken.** KV measures
0.037 MiB/token here, so a 262,144-token pool needs about 9.5 GiB against the
6.31 GiB that `gpu_memory_utilization` 0.80 yielded. Raising utilisation toward
0.88 is the obvious next arm, with 17.56 GiB of weights leaving little slack.
Not run.

Nothing is adopted. The shadow unit was removed and the shipped `:latest` pin is
unchanged; to re-run this, shadow the unit and swap the tag in `Image=` and the
`ExecStartPre` pull check. The compile cache from this run persists in
`~/.local/share/vllm/cache`, so a repeat starts warm.
