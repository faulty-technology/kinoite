# radiance at dfdfa38, and the cold-start floor

## What was measured

Two things in one GPU window.

**The pin bump.** radiance-vllm-mxfp4 `9735329` → `dfdfa38` (2026-09-18) picks up
`1d76c82`, which builds the DFlash2 drafter's int2 head only after the head's weight
is shared in, instead of sniffing it for zeros. Upstream measured the failure it
closes as acceptance length 7.63 → 1.01 and decode 320 → 47 tok/s, silently, with
GSM8K unmoved.

`serve-mxfp4.sh DRY_RUN=1` at both commits, diffed, on this box:

- **Five new container variables, all inert at TP=2:** `RADIANCE_AR_QBITS=6`,
  `RADIANCE_FP8_STREAM_TP1=1`, `RADIANCE_GDN_FUSED_MAX_ITEMS=32`,
  `RADIANCE_GDN_TRACE_SIDX=0`, `RADIANCE_GDN_LAZY=0`.
- **One new patch:** `patch_ar_qbits.py`, inert at 6 bits. `patch_gdn_lazy.py` also
  appears, but only behind `RADIANCE_GDN_LAZY=1`.
- **No change** to the cache suffix (`w4a8-093-gdnm-nqft-fp8s-gnq`), the `vllm serve`
  arguments, `setup-mxfp4.sh`, or the libr4d key at TP=2 (`b9e42ab-rx6`).
- **`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` is now `0..n-1`** instead of the absolute ids. On north both
  are `0,1`.

The regenerated `radiance.env` matches the new dry run exactly, apart from the three
variables it deliberately leaves out (the GPU indices and `R4D_SO`).

**The memory ceiling.** The 0.55 floor in
[runs/2026-09-15-radiance-quadlet-floor](2026-09-15-radiance-quadlet-floor.md) came up
on its first attempt. Here each ceiling was started with the compile cache cleared,
as `radiance.md` directs after an update, and then warm.

## Numbers

| ceiling | start | KV memory | KV pool | per 131,072 request | VRAM used per card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.55 | cold, first attempt | 2.69 GiB | — | **failed**: needs 2.79 GiB | — |
| 0.55 | warm (automatic restart) | 3.16 GiB | 135,795 | 1.04x | 17,786 MiB |
| 0.57 | cold, first attempt | 3.32 GiB | 155,869 | 1.19x | 18,221 MiB |
| 0.57 | warm | 3.53 GiB | 165,906 | 1.27x | 18,442 MiB |

Of 32,624 MiB per card. Times to healthy: 0.55 took 330 s including the failed
attempt, 0.57 took 195 s cold and 125 s warm.

**Drafter at dfdfa38.** The boot log reads `INT2_DRAFT_HEAD: deferred to first use`,
then builds the int2 head lazily. One greedy code prompt, thinking off: 263 tokens,
`finish_reason` `stop`, a correct iterative Fibonacci function, and 229 tokens accepted
over 34 drafts. That is 6.7 accepted per draft, not the ~0 of the failure.

All 47 patch steps reported `OK`, none `FAIL` or `NOOP`. The thinking-off patch applied.

## What it means

**The pin bump changes nothing at TP=2 except the drafthead fix.** It is a correctness
fix for a failure this box never hit: acceptance across eleven real-use boots on
2026-09-20/22 held at a mean of 3.2–4.3 per window, read from the journal and not a
controlled measurement.

**0.55 is below the floor for a cold start.** Compiling from an empty cache costs
0.47 GiB more at profiling time than a warm start, and at 0.55 that pushes the pool
under one 131,072-token request. A start after every update would fail once, then
succeed on `Restart=always`. The 09-15 floor was a warm start, so it did not see
this.

**0.57 clears it with margin.** Cold, it has 0.53 GiB over the requirement. Warm, it
holds 165,906 tokens and leaves about 13.8 GiB free per card, against 14.5 at 0.55.

Not measured: decode and prefill at dfdfa38 and 0.57. The 09-15 floor found decode
within 1% of the pinned configuration at 0.55, and nothing on the TP=2 path changed.
Also not measured: whether 0.56 would also clear a cold start.
