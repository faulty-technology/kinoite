# R9V is dropped

## Decision

The R9V stack is removed from the north image. R9V served Qwen3.8 Flash Next at
IQ4_XS on kyuz0's patched vLLM, on :8004.

Removed:
- `build_files/profiles/north/r9v.sh` and its call in `build.sh`. That takes out
  `r9v.container`, `kinoite-r9v-fetch` and the AMD SMI fix layer's Containerfile.
- The runbook `docs/how-to/r9v.md`, shipped as `/usr/share/kinoite/r9v.md`.
- `r9v.service` from `kinoite-llm-sleep`'s stop and restore lists.

The same model is now served by `radiance-flash`, StillDeadcode's `.rad` build
on the radiance engine.

## Why

`radiance-flash` beats R9V on every axis R9V was measured on, using the same
model:

| | R9V | radiance-flash |
|---|---|---|
| decode | 77–83 tok/s | 190–202 tok/s, single stream, 8K to 128K |
| sequences | 1 | 8 |
| context | 131,072 | 200,000 |
| first load, cold | 472 s | serving in 45 s |

Sources: [runs/2026-09-13-r9v-flash-next](../runs/2026-09-13-r9v-flash-next.md)
and [runs/2026-11-08-radiance-1170-flash-next](../runs/2026-11-08-radiance-1170-flash-next.md).
The two figures come from different engines, months apart, at a 250 W and a
235 W cap, so the ratio is approximate. The gap is still well over 2×.

R9V also carried its own upkeep:
- a locally built image, with an AMD SMI fix layer over a pinned upstream tag;
- a ~90 GiB model fetch plus a 27 GiB extracted embedding table;
- a GPU-index helper.

None of that is shared with any other stack.

Alternatives rejected:

- **Keep R9V as a fallback for Flash Next.** It would be a second engine for
  the same model, with less than half the speed and one sequence. If radiance
  breaks, the 27B on lemonade or vLLM is the fallback, not R9V.
- **Keep the unit but stop maintaining it.** An unmaintained unit pinned to an
  old toolbox still ships a runbook that reads as supported.

## What this does not settle

**Files on north are not removed by the image.** They are:
- `~/.local/share/models/r9v/`, the model and embedding table, 118 GB;
- `~/.local/share/r9v/cache`, 4.8 GB;
- the images `localhost/kinoite-r9v:latest`, `localhost/r9v-rocm-10.0:amdsmi`
  and `docker.io/kyuz0/amd-r9700-toolboxes:r9v-rocm-10.0`, about 21 GB, sharing
  most of their layers.

Delete them by hand when ready.

**Quality was never compared between the two builds:** R9V's IQ4_XS GGUF
against radiance's 4-bit codebook experts with an int8 trunk.
