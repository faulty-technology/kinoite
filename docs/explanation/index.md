- [Why llama.cpp is the decode path](engine-choice.md) — Two engines are built for this box and only one can run at a time: lemonade (llama.cpp) and vLLM + Open WebUI. lemonade Q8XL holds ~22 GB per card and vLLM FP8 needs ~28 GB of 32 GB, so they cannot coexist — every comparison behind this page is sequential for that reason.
- [GPU power, fans, and why tuning here is hard](gpu-power-and-fans.md) — Three findings shape every tuning decision on this box: the OverDrive karg has to be applied a specific way, nothing in the OverDrive table survives an idle cycle, and the fan floor is firmware.
- [Why `-sm tensor`, and what it costs](llama-cpp-tensor-split.md) — llama.cpp's tensor split is the single largest decode lever on this box: +44.4% on the 27B with the MTP head, and the difference between beating vLLM everywhere and losing to it on short prompts ([runs/2026-08-30-tensor-split](../runs/2026-08-30-tensor-split.md), [runs/2026-08-30-quant-sweep](../runs/2026-08-30-quant-sweep.md)).
- [Rootless Quadlets and SELinux confinement](quadlets-and-selinux.md) — The two silently-failing constraints on this box: rootless Quadlets must ship in /etc (podman 5.8.4 ignores the /usr search path), and SELinux confinement of the containers.
- [Choosing a quant](quant-selection.md) — The seeded recipes are Q6_K with one deliberate IQ4_XS exception. The reasoning is one measurement: **the weight-size axis is flat above ~25 GB** ([runs/2026-08-30-quant-sweep](../runs/2026-08-30-quant-sweep.md)).
- [Why the Sunshine setup looks the way it does](sunshine-capture.md) — Why each non-obvious piece of sunshine.sh exists — e.g. the display cable decides which GPU owns the compositor and VAAPI device, so a dummy plug on the motherboard HDMI made it encode on the iGPU.
- [Suspend, resume, and waking the box](suspend-and-wake.md) — Two mechanisms that have to be understood together: the LLM suspend hook exists because S3 with a model loaded hangs the box, and Wake-on-WLAN only recovers a box that suspended *correctly*.
- [Where vLLM's decode time goes](vllm-decode-budget.md) — A measured breakdown of where vLLM's decode time goes — the non-speculative baseline is 24.3 tok/s (~41 ms/token), split into FP8 GEMVs, all-reduce and an unexplained remainder.
- [vLLM KV-cache group padding — why it happens, and the DFlash2 trap](vllm-kv-cache-padding.md) — Why vLLM pads KV-cache layer groups (uneven buckets get placeholder layers) and why the DFlash2 drafter's 'Add 3 padding layers' warning is already optimal, not a misconfiguration.
